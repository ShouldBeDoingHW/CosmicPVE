# Cleanup candidate: manual acceptance checklist

Run in a NeoForge 1.21.11 development client with operator permission. `/cosmic` subcommands are permission-gated. These are real registered surfaces; visual acceptance is still pending. Use an ordinary inventory/chest and `/tinkerer` for the UI steps. Do not use this checklist as evidence that a human visual check has occurred.

1. Give a Cosmic Book with `/cosmic enchant book give @s cosmicpve:molten 1 100 1`, apply it to compatible armor, and verify there is no text above the hotbar. Repeat with `/cosmic tinkerer dust give @s unique 1` dragged onto a matching examined book; success must still change by +1 percentage point without an action-bar message.
2. Test another item application with `/cosmic accessory give belt_socket @s 100` and `/give @s minecraft:diamond_leggings`; apply the socket and verify no action-bar text. Existing sound and normal UI behavior should remain.
3. Use `/cosmic skin give @s cosmicpve:seasons_beatings` and inspect the item: title `Item Skin (Season's Beatings)`, red effect lines, a blank row, gray italic `Attach this skin to any SWORD` with white underlined SWORD, `to over-ride its visual appearance.`, then the separated attach/detach instructions. Use `/cosmic skin give @s cosmicpve:boosted_chainsaw` for the corresponding AXE wording; attach each only to its valid weapon class.
4. Use `/cosmic mask give @s cosmicpve:dragon`, `/cosmic accessory give blood_diamond @s`, and `/cosmic accessory give shock_therapy @s`; inspect Mask/Amulet/Belt tooltips for red `#FF5555` effect descriptions and unchanged identity/name colors. Apply socketed Amulet/Belt and confirm their mechanics remain unchanged.
5. Inspect Shock Therapy and Bandolier (`/cosmic accessory give bandolier @s`) in inventory, hotbar, and a normal chest GUI. The visible face should no longer be reversed. Attach each to socketed leggings and confirm the worn position/orientation did not change. Jelly Roll and Cinderwolf fallback belt icons should remain normal. This is a human-visual sign-off point.
6. Give multiple enchanted eligible armor/weapon pieces (the existing `/cosmic reward equipment sample @s ultimate armor 8` operator fixture is suitable), place three in `/tinkerer`, and confirm. The outputs should be **one** stored-XP Bottle with summed value, not three Bottles. Repeat with examined Books in the same confirmation: Dust remains separate; check a full inventory for safe overflow delivery. Cosmetic-attached gear should still be rejected.
7. Use `/cosmic tinkerer dust give @s simple 1` and repeat for unique, elite, ultimate, legendary, mastery and heroic. Every Dust rarity should glint and show exactly `Drag n' drop onto a matching rarity enchantment book to add +1% success rate!` Apply one to a matching book and check that Destroy Rate is unchanged.
8. Use `/cosmic vkit give @s phoenix` and repeat with ogre, judgement and slayer. Each Crystal should have one blank tooltip line between kit lore and the right-click level-up instruction.
9. Use `/cosmic containers memory-chest @s` and `/cosmic reward secret-weapon-cache give @s`; both **container items** should glint. Open them normally to check existing animation and one-result behavior.
10. While holding an unopened Cache, use `/cosmic reward secret-weapon-cache force rangers_bow`, then open it; inspect Power V, Unbreaking III, Infinity I and Flame I, with no Mending. Repeat with fresh Caches and `force phantom_scythe` / `force travelers_space_blaster`; inspect Mending I and their retained Sharpness or Quick Charge/Piercing. The underlying six-way Cache pool remains equal-weight.
11. Use `/cosmic space-chest give @s ultimate 1`, then legendary and mastery. Their flavor line should be `#C7C7C7`; tier name colors should remain distinct. Open a Chest and check that reveal animation and close/reopen recovery preserve the same final reward.
12. Sample the production generated rows without repeatedly opening random Chests: `/cosmic reward equipment sample @s ultimate armor 8`, `/cosmic reward equipment sample @s ultimate weapon 8`, and the same commands with `legendary` and `mastery`. Armor must be Iron; weapon types should include Diamond Sword, Diamond Axe, Bow and Crossbow over repeated samples (population ratio 3:3:2:2). Each item should have one coherent non-italic custom name and valid Cosmic enchants: Ultimate 1–3 up to Ultimate; Legendary 2–4 up to Legendary; Mastery 4–5 up to **Legendary**, or fewer only if the applicable ordinary pool is smaller. No Mastery/Heroic/exclusive enchants on generated gear.
13. Inspect vanilla packages on those samples: armor Protection IV+Unbreaking III; Sword Sharpness V+Unbreaking III+Fire Aspect II; Axe Sharpness V+Unbreaking III; Bow Power V+Unbreaking III+Infinity I+Flame I; Crossbow Quick Charge III+Unbreaking III without Piercing/Multishot. Mastery adds Mending I except on Bow. Confirm no Mending on Ultimate/Legendary.
14. Use `/cosmic reward inspect cosmicpve:space_chest/ultimate` (and legendary/mastery) to inspect the two distinct generated-equipment rows and compact Tier Trinket row. Use `/cosmic reward roll cosmicpve:conquest 100` or a Space Chest table for a sample of actual Tier Trinkets; verify all five real families appear at the correct tier. Use `/cosmic containers trials-creation-kit @s`, open several Kits and check five rewards with no duplicate physical expanded row; Insurance is possible. An operator can also inspect the physical type/value with `/cosmic trial portal inspect` after applying a Trinket to a Portal from `/cosmic trial portal give @s`.

15. Enter Pitch Perfect with 1–4 players and verify the fixed 4/5/6/7 success requirement. After one or more successes, take a wrong portal: the whole active party returns to spawn, exactly one completed round is lost (never below zero), and all six notes/target reroll. Each correct answer, including final, plays one rapid five-note ascending PLING cue for participants only.
16. Enter a first and later physical Trial room while damaged and hungry, including equipment that raises effective max HP. Verify full current effective HP and `/restore` food/saturation after the room loadout, with no heal proc, chat or action-bar feedback. Skipping an ordinal, opening a Madness ballot and choosing DEAL must not count as a physical-room restore.
17. Activate Inventory Shuffle, Owl Gene, Statues and Rocket Man in ROOM_ACTIVE. Each should show 3/2/1 in a single `#8C1708`-name action bar with one ascending PLING per second, aligned to the existing event. Overlaps should show both names/counts together. Verify silence and cleared warning on Decision, death, disconnect and cleanup; ordinary Cosmic application paths remain action-bar-free.
18. Summon Cosmic Ranger in a normal authored arena and at the 66%/33% thresholds confirm four complete safe jockey pairs. Deliberately obstruct one Light-marker spawn in a disposable test world: its pair should relocate nearby inside the arena without suffocation. Outside Woodlands, confirm the cardinal fallback also avoids solid terrain. Never edit arena blocks just to satisfy a wave.
19. During an active Dense Woodlands Adventure, wear a complete Ranger set: its bonus should work. Replace it with complete Phantom/Yeti/Engineer sets and verify bonuses stay suppressed. Mask, Amulet, Belt and skin gameplay effects remain disabled, while all item components stay attached and behave normally again after exiting; only Simple/Unique/Elite ordinary Cosmic enchant effects are permitted.
20. Give a Jelly Roll Belt with `/cosmic accessory give jelly_roll @s`. Inspect its donut-and-strap model in inventory, hotbar, and a chest GUI, then attach it to socketed leggings and check the worn waist position from front, rear and side. The authored texture/model is wired, but actual in-game fit still needs human visual acceptance.
21. Inspect Grim Axe and The Carver skin items: Grim Axe should say `5% chance to suppress enemy unique and elite enchantments.`; The Carver should show `Devour IV` and its separate damage effect on two distinct lines. Inspect an ordinary rated Black Scroll: a blank line should follow `Returned Book Success Rate`, and another should follow the line ending `returns it as a book.`

When done, record any visual discrepancy (especially Belt GUI/worn poses), exact item/seed where practical, and whether the loaded world used third-party resource packs. Do not mark this candidate accepted solely on automated tests.
